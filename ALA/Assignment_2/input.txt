Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) partnered with Microsoft to strengthen artificial intelligence and digital innovation across its academic ecosystem. Microsoft Labs planned across five constituent units will give students and faculty opportunities for hands-on learning, experimentation, interdisciplinary research, innovation, and industry engagement.

Manipal University Jaipur organised its Internal Smart India Hackathon 2025, with more than 400 student teams working on over 100 problem statements. Around 3,000 students participated in challenges involving technology, sustainability, healthcare, smart automation, education, and societal issues.

MAHE hosted IP2E360, a one-day workshop bringing researchers, innovators, and entrepreneurs together to explore how intellectual property can be translated into innovation, enterprise, and societal impact. Topics included patents, publications, commercialisation, licensing, and the development of intellectual wealth.

The Manipal Centre for Biotherapeutics Research introduced its M.Sc. (Research) students to institute facilities and MAHE support systems during an orientation programme. Students visited facilities including the Health Sciences Library, Marena, and innovation hubs such as MUTBI and the Manipal-GOK Bio-Incubator.
